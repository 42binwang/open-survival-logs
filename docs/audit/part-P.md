## Verification notes

- **Whole-run probes for the ending days.** The unit tests for route endings (`tests/endings.test.js`, the
  "resolves on Day N after the final wave" family) preset `TAG_FINAL_WAVE_SURVIVED` at 22:00 the day before, so they
  cannot see when a real final horde ends. I re-ran the `tests/storyrun.test.js` harness (every system ticking, the
  real horde calendar) for two committed routes: The Truth (Warehouse Manager) ended on **Day 85** at 05:52 after the
  `story-84` final horde; Side By Side (Wage Slave) ended on **Day 87** at 05:34 after the `story-86` final horde.
  The committed `storyrun` test itself only asserts "Day 84 or 85" for The Truth. See the P06 / P09 gaps.
- **Route prerequisites match the config notes** in `src/data/gen/achievements.js` (Config_Achievement notes): Fortress
  = reinforcement ≥ 600, door ≥ 2000, windows ≥ 1500, 4 × 36002 / 36003 / 36004, 80 % reserve on Days 60–69; Net = 6
  survivors alive at the Day 71 check; Supply Station = 1200 / 180 / 8 at the same check; Greenhouse = 24 planters at
  the Day 71 check. The titanium door (30002) and bulletproof window (35002) have exactly 2000 / 1500 max HP.
- **Cross-system wiring checked by the new tests**: the social system really sets the tags the Girl / Companionship
  checks read (`TAG_*_RESCUE2/3_COMPLETE`, `rescueStage`, hearts) and starts the third rescue only after the kit is
  committed; the Day 71 network snapshot (`TAG_STRANGER_NETWORK_OK`) and drone drops (`camp.prep.supply`) feed the Net;
  the Fortress power samples read the power system's `state.power.capacity` / `stored`; the horde system moves the
  final horde to 86 / 84 and leaves Evacuate on 87; the veteran's letter and the Truth's records room count toward
  Puzzle (A:1203); the Supply promise events hand out the three slips for A:1223; `storyEndless` sets EndlessState 2
  (A:2402) and endless hordes get scheduled.
- **One route per save** is enforced per run (it survives save / load); a rebirth starts the next loop with no route,
  which matches G2's advice to take a different ending in an earlier loop. The confirmation dialog in
  `src/ui/eventPanel.js` ('commitConfirm') says "this save can never choose another path", which overstates it.
- **UI not browser-checked**: the event modal and reward chips (P01 / P02) and the ending screen (P14) were verified by
  reading `src/ui/eventPanel.js` and `src/ui/endingScreen.js`; the data they render is covered by the tests above.
- **Fidelity notes that do not change a rating**: the Supply Station's special trading point (`p_supplyPoint`) and the
  Net's camp run (`p_campRun`) are event choices that cost food and need no drone (the Net also counts real drone
  drops); Evacuate additionally asks for three beacon inspections on different days after the commitment
  (`promise_evacuate`), which G2 does not list; a route can be committed before Day 70 as soon as it is open and the
  kit is at home.

## Gaps

- **P06 — the "Day 86" ending lands on Day 87 in real play.** A committed route's final horde is scheduled at 22:00 on
  `ROUTES[route].finalWaveDay` (`src/sim/horde.js` hordeEntry / syncFinalHorde, `NIGHT_HOUR = 22`). Its six waves need
  well over two hours to spawn and clear (the probe's `story-86` wave ended at 05:34), so `TAG_FINAL_WAVE_SURVIVED` is
  set after midnight and `dueEnding` in `src/sim/endings.js` fires on Day 87; the ending screen header and the rebirth
  memory then say "Day 87". Fix, either: (a) start a committed route's final wave the night before its ending day
  (`finalWaveDay: endingDay - 1` in `src/content/endings.js` ROUTES, keeping the default Day 87 horde for uncommitted
  runs and Evacuate), or (b) in `triggerEnding` (`src/sim/endings.js`) record `state.run.endingDay = ENDINGS[id].day`
  when a route ending resolves the morning after its final wave. Add a whole-run assertion to `tests/storyrun.test.js`.
  The same off-by-one hits Fortress, Greenhouse, Companionship and Supply Station (86 → 87) and Net (87 → 88); those
  rows do not state a day, so they are not downgraded, but the same fix covers them.
- **P09 — the "Day 84" ending lands on Day 85 in real play.** Same mechanism as P06: the Truth's final horde
  (`story-84`) starts at 22:00 on Day 84 and the ending was recorded on Day 85 at 05:52 in the probe;
  `tests/storyrun.test.js` › 'committing to the Truth moves the final horde to Day 84 and the ending follows once it is
  beaten' accepts 84–85. Same fix as P06. Everything else in the row (three hospital records and the recorder brought
  home, the recorder set up, the kit used on it, the final horde moved to Day 84) is implemented and tested.
- **P10 — "warm house" only checks that heaters are installed.** `heaterCount` / `allFloorsHeated` in
  `src/sim/story.js` count any installed heater with `f.on !== false`, so an unpowered AC or electric heater, or an
  unlit fuel heater (65001), satisfies it, and no floor temperature is read; G2 says every floor must actually reach
  temperature. Verified: with an unpowered AC as the only basement heater, `allFloorsHeated()` is true while
  `heatingByFloor()` in `src/sim/weather.js` reports no heat on B1. Fix: in `allFloorsHeated`, require
  `heatingByFloor(state)[floor]` to have `electric + fuel > 0` (or `indoorTemp(state, floor) >= THERMOSTAT`, 18 °C) on
  every unlocked floor. The rest of the row (24 planters at the Day 71 check, 24 flowers and 36 produce harvested
  after the commitment) is implemented and tested.
- **P14 — UI: needs browser check.** The illustration (`SCENES[def.scene]` gradient plus emoji art) and the Safehouse
  Snapshot's one photo per floor (`snapshotFloor` renders each floor with an offscreen `IsoRenderer` into a PNG) exist
  only in `src/ui/endingScreen.js` (showEnding, snapshots) and cannot run under Node. Check in a browser: reach an
  ending (e.g. `triggerEnding(game.state, 'lastOne')`) and confirm the three pages render, there is one image per
  floor with never-unlocked floors labelled "never opened", and the tally, score table and challenge list match
  `safehouseTally`, `survivalScore` and `challengeResults`.
