# WP-P0-15 — Balance: scavenging and the forager's horde nights (BUG-0057, BUG-0058)

**Role:** sim and balance (a fresh agent). **Phase:** P0. **Depends on:** WP-P0-11 (merged at #13: the balance bots,
bands and `docs/BALANCE.md`). The balance check is full-tier and required from the P0 sign-off, so this lands before
checkpoint 1.

## Why
The balance bots play the real sim, 50 seeds per difficulty (`docs/balance/P0.md`, `P0.json`). 20 of 26 P0 bands
hold. The six that fail are the forager's:
- `forager-gain-relaxed`, `-normal`, `-hard`, `-outOfAmmo`: the forager lives 0, 0, −0.5 and −1 days longer than the
  prepper, against a band of +2 to +14 days (https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3782585359: scavenging feeds you, it doesn't
  replace farming). BUG-0057.
- `forager-horde25-normal`, `-hard`: it survives the Day 25 horde in 86% of runs on Normal (band ≥ 90%) and 51% on
  Hard (band ≥ 75%; 36 of 47 breached). BUG-0058.

The bug entries only suspect causes: exploring brings home too little food for its time and stamina; days spent
exploring leave the openings unrepaired before the horde. Neither has been shown.

## Scope
1. **Root cause first, with evidence.** Trace seeded forager runs day by day: when it goes out and where, what it
   brings home (in days of food), what it spends (time, stamina, food, hp), and what it misses at home (repairs,
   cooking, farming). Establish which of these the gap comes from:
   - the sim deviating from the source: loot tables, container contents, search times, stamina costs, zombie density
     at the sites the bot visits. Compare with the extracted config (`src/data/gen`) and `research/`;
   - the bot's policy not doing what `docs/BALANCE.md` defines the forager to do;
   - the band itself, only if the source contradicts it.
2. **Fix at the root.**
   - A sim fix restores the source's behaviour (config values, rules) and adds unit tests that pin it. Existing sim
     tests stay as they are.
   - A bot fix makes the policy match its `docs/BALANCE.md` definition. It is a change to a gate tool, so it must not
     make the bands easier than the definition allows: no perfect information, no play the definition doesn't
     describe.
   - A band change needs a source citation, the quality-standards reviewer's written approval and a line in
     `docs/balance/bands-log.jsonl`. It never loosens a band without a source.
3. **Re-run** `npm run balance` (50 seeds per difficulty) and regenerate `docs/balance/P0.md` and `P0.json` through
   the tool: all 26 P0 bands hold, no crash, every save/load round trip identical.
4. **Close BUG-0057 and BUG-0058** with their root causes. The integrator records each closure from the reviewer's
   verdict.

## Exclusive paths
- `tools/balance/bots.mjs`, only the forager and what it shares with the other bots, and only to match
  `docs/BALANCE.md`.
- The sim files the root cause needs. Name them in your report; nobody else edits `src/sim` during P0 except through
  hook patches. Shared files (`src/contracts/`, `src/main.js`, `src/systems.js`, config) go as hook patches.
- Tests you add; `docs/balance/forager.md` (the root-cause write-up); `docs/balance/P0.md` and `P0.json`, through the
  tool only.

## Acceptance
- `docs/balance/forager.md` shows the root cause with seeded traces and source citations.
- All 26 P0 bands hold with 50 seeds, and the gate's `balance` check passes.
- No existing test deleted or weakened; new tests pin the fix.
- The quality-standards reviewer approves the root cause and any bot or band change.
