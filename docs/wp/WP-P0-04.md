# WP-P0-04 — Look reference and art direction (docs/ART.md)

**Role:** art lane (art director). **Phase:** P0. **Depends on:** nothing (ffmpeg is installed system-wide).

## Scope
- `tools/targets/fetch-targets.mjs`: download the Steam store screenshots (URLs in `research/store_appdetails.json`)
  and extract trailer frames from the HLS stream (ffmpeg) — evenly sampled plus every distinct shot — into
  `assets/targets/source/` with a `manifest.json` (source URL, time code, SHA-256). Look reference only: never traced,
  never shipped, never part of the build.
- Analysis of the references, written up in `docs/ART.md`:
  - **camera:** projection (orthographic, or narrow-FOV perspective with its FOV), pitch and yaw, framing (how many
    tiles / how much of a floor is in view at 1080p), zoom range — decided and justified with measurements;
  - **scale and texel density:** units (1 unit = 1 m), wall and door heights, tile size, texel density targets for
    hero and regular surfaces;
  - **lighting rigs per time of day** (dawn, day, dusk, night with lamps, blackout, storm): sun or moon direction,
    color temperature, intensity, sky and bounce, interior lamp color and falloff;
  - **tone mapping and grading:** AgX, exposure, a color-grading LUT (write it as a `.cube` in
    `assets/targets/lut/` derived from the references), grain and vignette strength;
  - **value and saturation bands** per material family (plaster walls, wood floors, tiles, fabric, metal, glass,
    foliage, skin, blood, UI accents), measured from the references in linear and sRGB;
  - reference boards with annotated crops (`docs/art/`), mood boards from the image tool marked concept-only.
- Measurement script(s) for the bands (`tools/targets/measure.py` or `.mjs`).

## Exclusive paths
`tools/targets/`, `assets/targets/`, `docs/ART.md`, `docs/art/` except the lane folders (`docs/art/<lane>/` — materials, characters, furniture, homes, icons, ui, spike — belong to their lanes).

## Read
Everything (especially `research/`, `src/content/homes.js`, `src/render/iso.js`).

## Acceptance
- ≥ 8 screenshots and ≥ 24 trailer frames with manifest and hashes; `docs/ART.md` answers every bullet above with
  numbers; the LUT file loads (valid `.cube`).

## Deliverables
The files above on `wp/P0-04`, the JSON report.
