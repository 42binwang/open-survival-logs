# WP-P0-11 — Quality rubric, art metrics and ledgers

**Role:** architect (quality). **Phase:** P0. **Depends on:** WP-P0-01 (gate check registry), WP-P0-04 (ART.md
bands), WP-P0-14 (`tools/lib/ssim.mjs` and `tools/lib/png.mjs`). Immutable after P0 except demonstrated-bug fixes
approved by a reviewer.

## Scope
- `docs/QUALITY.md`: the 12-axis rubric (match to approved targets, lighting, materials, models, characters and
  animation, VFX, UI clarity and consistency, feedback and feel, onboarding, audio mix and music, writing and
  localization, stability and performance), each scored 1–5 with concrete descriptors per score and the evidence a
  reviewer must attach; the side-by-side protocol (in-engine vs closest source screenshot at matching framing, per
  home and exploration site); thresholds (every axis ≥ 4 to pass P1, P3, P4; scores never decrease);
  `docs/quality/scores.jsonl` format.
- `tools/art-metrics.mjs`: PBR validity (albedo in physical range, roughness not saturated, unit-length normals),
  seamless tiling and repetition, texel density ±10% of ART.md, triangle budgets, icon distinctness per category
  (perceptual hash distance), text-artifact detection in renders, lightmap seam detection, required animation clips
  present — each a check the gate calls, run over the asset manifests. The material checks in
  `tools/materials/checks.mjs` (P0-05: value bands, roughness range, unit normals, seamless tiling, KTX2 decoding)
  are importable; reuse them rather than re-implementing.
- `tools/visual/`: SSIM comparison against baselines (`tests/visual/baselines/`), with a change log. It uses the
  shared SSIM in `tools/lib/ssim.mjs` and PNG codec in `tools/lib/png.mjs` (WP-P0-14) rather than a second
  implementation, and the camera from `src/contracts/look.js`.
- `tools/budgets/`: Playwright budget runner (frame p95 / worst, draw calls, GPU memory, download size, cold / warm
  first-interactive, asset total, 100-day headless sim time) at 1920×1080 with 4× CPU throttling.
- Ledgers: `docs/bugs.jsonl` (schema, severity S1–S4), `docs/playtests/README.md` (personas, protocol),
  `docs/BALANCE.md` (bands per difficulty derived from `research/` guides and the current sim: days survived,
  deaths by cause, food stock curve, horde outcomes, points per loop) and `tools/balance/` (headless bots, 50 seeds
  per difficulty).

## Exclusive paths
`docs/QUALITY.md`, `docs/quality/`, `tools/art-metrics.mjs`, `tools/art-metrics/`, `tools/visual/`, `tools/budgets/`,
