# WP-P0-06 — 60-second audio test

**Role:** audio lane. **Phase:** P0. **Depends on:** nothing (ffmpeg and sox are installed system-wide).

## Scope
- **Music in code** (`tools/music/`): compose MIDI programmatically (a small score DSL or MIDI writer), rendered offline
  with CC0 instrument samples (VSCO 2 Community Edition, VCSL) through a sampler you write (SFZ-style mapping,
  velocity layers, round robins) — no DAW. Layers for the phases: pre-outbreak, day, night, horde, ending; the test
  needs at least the night and horde layers and a transition between them. Master to −16 LUFS integrated
  (measure with ffmpeg `ebur128` / `loudnorm`).
- **Foley and SFX** (`tools/audio/`): sources Kenney audio packs (CC0), procedural synthesis, and the Sonniss GDC
  bundles only if downloadable without an account (it answers 403 to scripted requests today — try the official
  bundle links; document the fallback if it stays unavailable). One shared processing chain: EQ, room reverb per
  space (apartment, stairwell, shop, basement, outdoors), loudness normalization. Every repeated sound ≥ 3 variants
  with pitch and volume variation: footsteps (wood, tile), door knock and bang, zombie groan, cooking sizzle, rain,
  thunder, generator, UI click / confirm / error, pickup.
- **The test itself:** a 60-second scripted sequence (apartment at night → rain → a zombie at the door → the horde
  layer → quiet), mixed with buses (master, music, sfx, ambience, UI) and ducking, rendered to
  `assets/audio/test/audio-test-60s.ogg`, plus `pages/audio-test.html` that plays it live through WebAudio with
  spatialized in-world sources (PannerNode) and the bus faders.
- `docs/AUDIO.md`: the chain, buses, loudness targets, variant rules, naming.
- Added after dispatch (WP-P0-14 froze it later): the audio manifest follows the rebuild contract in
  `src/contracts/assets.js`, with a `rebuild` recipe (entry, args, inputs, pinned tools, fixed seeds, `cost`) and, on
  every entry, its recipe name and the SHA-256 `digests` of each shipped file. The re-bake compares a log-frequency
  spectrogram at SSIM ≥ 0.99, duration within 10 ms and loudness within 0.5 LU, so renders must be deterministic.

## Exclusive paths
`tools/music/`, `tools/audio/`, `assets/audio/`, `pages/audio-test.html`, `docs/AUDIO.md`,
`assets/lock/WP-P0-06.json`, `assets/credits/WP-P0-06.md`, `tests/audio-assets.test.js`.

## Read
Everything.

## Acceptance
- The render is 60 s ± 1 s, music stems measure −16 LUFS ± 1 integrated, no clipping (true peak ≤ −1 dBTP).
- Every repeated sound has ≥ 3 variants (`tests/audio-assets.test.js`); all sources are in the lock fragment.
