# WP-P0-10 — Furniture for the spike room

**Role:** furniture lane. **Phase:** P0. **Depends on:** Blender; WP-P0-05 materials (use its library when it lands;
start with the builders meanwhile); WP-P0-14 (material binding and rebuild contract; the manifest adopts them when
they land).

## Scope
- Models for every piece in the Wage Slave's 1F living room starter set (see `src/content/homes.js` apartment
  starter list and the config furniture it references): sofa, coffee table, TV (wall), medium shelf, small storage
  rack, workbench (broken and repaired variants), wood pile, newspaper stacks, flowerpot, radio, windows and the
  front door — a CC0 Poly Haven model where one fits (re-materialed with our library), otherwise parametric builders
  in Blender (`tools/blender/furniture/`) with bevels and weighted normals, normal and AO maps baked from a high-poly
  version onto the in-game mesh, plus the curvature bake the material library's wear convention needs
  (`assets/materials/library.json` → `library.conventions`).
- Budgets: furniture ≤ 5k triangles, props ≤ 1.5k, hero pieces ≤ 10k; LODs where needed; damage states as variants.
- Sizes per ART.md §2: the furniture tile-fill rule and shelf and rack heights. Doors and windows are wall openings,
  exempt from the fill rule.
- **Openings belong to the shell (coordinator decision, matching WP-P0-13's manifest):** frames, jambs, thresholds and
  sills are the shell's. The door (config 211) ships only its leaf, lock, levers, peephole and hinge knuckles under
  `leaf_hinge`; the window (config 212) ships its sashes, glass, gaskets and latch. Both are sized to the shell's
  clear opening and placed at the shell's wall centre.
- Output glTF + KTX2 under `assets/furniture/<family>/`, and `assets/furniture/manifest.json` mapping config furniture
  ids to model + variant (the coverage tool reads it).
- The manifest follows WP-P0-14's contract (`src/contracts/assets.js`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` "Assets"):
  - mesh parts bind library materials through `AssetEntry.materials`, with per-config overrides in
    `bindings[cfgId].materials`;
  - a `rebuild` recipe for the family (entry, args, inputs, pinned tools, fixed seeds, `cost`), and on every entry its
    recipe name and the SHA-256 `digests` of each shipped file. The gate's asset-lock check re-bakes a sample and
    fails any shipped file without them.
