# WP-P0-13 — Apartment shell and baked lightmaps

**Role:** environment lane. **Phase:** P0. **Depends on:** Blender; WP-P0-04 (ART.md scale and lighting rigs);
WP-P0-05 materials; WP-P0-14 (material binding and rebuild contract; the manifest adopts them when they land).

## Scope
- `tools/blender/shell/`: build the Wage Slave apartment shell by script from `src/content/homes.js` (floors, walls
  with real thickness, ceilings, doors and windows with frames, skirting and trim from a trim sheet, bevels, terrace
  and basement), 1 unit = 1 m, grid-aligned with the sim tiles.
- Bake static light in Cycles into lightmaps for 1F (the spike room first, then the whole floor), UV2 unwrapped,
  denoised, packed; light probes (SH) for movable furniture. Follow the bake and runtime contract in `docs/ART.md`
  §4.4:
  - linear sets DAY / DUSK / NIGHT / LAMPS plus a sky-only OVERCAST, which the renderer blends with per-rig weights.
    Per P0-04c, DUSK is baked in two passes, `DUSK_SKY` (sky plus its bounce) and `DUSK_SUN` (dusk-sun indirect plus
    its bounce), weighted so the ss_04 reference texel reads 0.197 in total with 13% from `DUSK_SUN` (the dusk
    interior is sky-lit, about 6900 K, not orange). Probes bake from the same sets;
  - DAY, DUSK and NIGHT hold sky, bounce and the key light's indirect light only. The key light's direct term (sun or
    moon) stays out of the bake (in Cycles, exclude the key's direct contribution), because the renderer adds it at
    runtime through the shadowed key light. Baking it too would render sunlit surfaces at about 1.9×;
  - LAMPS is its own switchable layer, so a blackout can turn it off;
  - hidden geometry (upper floors, roofs) stays in the bake as a shadow-only occluder.
- Walls 3.3 m (ART.md §2), confirmed with a native corner per home before baking; `keepFullHeight` on the walls
  ART.md §1.3 names; slab, ceiling, window and door sizes from ART.md §2.
- Export `assets/homes/apartment/1F.glb` (+ lightmap KTX2s) and `assets/homes/apartment/manifest.json`.
- The manifest follows WP-P0-14's contract: surfaces bind library materials through `AssetEntry.materials`; a
  `rebuild` recipe per family (shell, lightmaps) with `cycles { seed, samples }`, pinned tools and an honest `cost`,
  and on every entry its recipe name and the SHA-256 `digests` of each shipped file. The asset-lock check puts one
  asset of every family in each sample and fails up front when the recipe costs exceed its 50-minute budget, so a
  lightmap re-bake has to fit comfortably inside that.
