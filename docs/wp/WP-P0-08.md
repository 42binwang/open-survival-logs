# WP-P0-08 — Tech spike: one furnished room in three.js at final quality

**Role:** render lane (hero). **Phase:** P0.
**Depends on:** WP-P0-01 (build + renderer interface), WP-P0-04 (ART.md), WP-P0-05 (materials), WP-P0-09 (survivor),
WP-P0-10 (furniture), WP-P0-13 (shell + lightmaps), WP-P0-14 (material binding), WP-P0-14b (placement and dressing
fields, `Renderer.stats()`).

## Scope
- `src/render3d/` implementing the renderer interface from `src/contracts/render.js`, consuming the view model
  (`src/contracts/view.js`) — no sim imports, no game logic.
- The spike: the living room of the Wage Slave's apartment (1F), furnished from the view model's furniture list,
  at final quality — scanned materials, baked lightmaps (day / dusk / night-with-lamps blended at runtime), light
  probes for movable furniture, one shadow-casting key light, dynamic point lights (lamps), GTAO, bloom, AgX tone
  mapping, the shared LUT, grain (ART.md: a ±0.5-code dither, since the source has no film grain) and vignette — by
  day and by night, with the survivor idle and walking (driven by the view model's player position and action).
- The camera per ART.md, read from `src/contracts/look.js` (WP-P0-14).
- Materials per object from the manifests: `resolveMaterials()` merges the entry's part bindings with
  `bindings[cfgId].materials`, and `instanceSeed(uid, part)` seeds the variation unless a seed is pinned.
- The material library's conventions (`assets/materials/library.json` → `library.conventions`: tint, macro
  variation, curvature wear and gravity dirt) implemented in the three.js materials, driven by the per-object
  binding from WP-P0-14; the furniture lane supplies the curvature bakes.
- The grade LUT (`survival-log-grade-v2.cube`, a neutral tint only) ships from `assets/post/`: `assets/targets/lut/`
  holds the reference fit and never ships. Re-fit it with `tools/targets/lut.py` against the first AgX spike renders
  and check the 0.18 grey card per rig within ±5 codes.
- Post and lighting per ART.md §4.4, §5.3 and §6: lightmap sets blended by rig weight, with the key light's direct
  term added at runtime only; the emitter layer drawn after the grade (lamp shades); the ±0.5-code dither; fog, bloom
  and GTAO as ART.md specifies; the vignette as a tunable `vignetteCorner` (provisional). Calibrate the LAMPS layer
  weight so the t033 reference wall reads 0.141 ±5 codes.
- The survivor's walk clip moves 1.18 m/s in place (`clip.userData.rootMotion.speedMps`), while the sim moves 5 m/s
  of real time at speed 1: define a playback-rate policy, and request a run clip from the character lane if walking
  at that speed looks wrong.
- `pages/spike.html` (or `?render=3d&spike=1`) with a day/night toggle; Playwright screenshots at 1920×1080 for day,
  dusk and night into `docs/art/spike/`; frame-time capture (p95, worst, draw calls, GPU memory) under 4× CPU
  throttling.

## Exclusive paths
`src/render3d/`, `pages/spike.html`, `tests/e2e/spike.spec.*`, `docs/art/spike/`, `assets/post/`.

## Read
Everything.

## Acceptance

  the Canvas renderer still works with `?render=2d`.

## Deliverables
The renderer spike, screenshots, the performance numbers, the JSON report.
