# WP-P0-09 — Survivor character and animation spike

**Role:** characters and animation lane. **Phase:** P0. **Depends on:** Blender (installed system-wide);
reads `docs/ART.md` when WP-P0-04 lands.

## Scope
- Base human from the MPFB (MakeHuman) CC0 system assets, fetched and pinned (lock fragment); the Wage Slave dressed
  as the source shows him (t035, t069, ss_04, the store header, the trailer opening): a dark charcoal hoodie with the
  hood down at the nape, faded mid-blue jeans, dark sneakers with white soles, short dark hair; no tie, no belt; values
  calibrated to the source on screen under the ART.md day-interior rig (hoodie about sRGB 45–50, jeans 40–80
  blue-grey, hair 40–60). Scanned-quality skin and cloth materials; rigged in Blender (MPFB rig or a game rig with
  the standard humanoid bone names). (Corrected after review: the first version of this file said white shirt,
  loosened tie and office shoes, which the source never shows.)
- Motion: CMU motion-capture clips retargeted in Blender, headless by script (`tools/blender/characters/`), loopable
  with root motion extracted; the pipeline must take any CMU clip. The source survivor runs at about 5 m/s, which
  matches the sim (1 tile per 12 game-seconds at speed 1), so the spike needs `idle`, `walk` and `run` (natural speed
  about 4.5–5 m/s). The CMU BVH mirror on cgspeed is gone — use the original ASF/AMC files from mocap.cs.cmu.edu
  (convert in the script) or another no-account mirror, pinned by hash.
- Export glTF (skinned, clips named `idle`, `walk`, `run`), meshopt-compressed, textures KTX2 at the ART.md hero
  texel density (512 px/m class; the character never exceeds about 230 px/m on screen), ≤ 15k triangles for the hero
  character, ≤ 4 bone influences.
- Turntable and game-zoom renders (`docs/art/characters/`) judged at game zoom: silhouette, motion, lighting —
  including the shipped glb rendered in three.js at the `src/contracts/look.js` camera (60° and 50°) under the ART.md
  day-interior and dusk rigs, side by side with the source crops.
- Added after dispatch (WP-P0-14 froze it later): `assets/characters/manifest.json` follows the rebuild contract in
  `src/contracts/assets.js`, with a `rebuild` recipe (entry, args, inputs, pinned tools, fixed seeds, `cost`) and, on
  every entry, its recipe name and the SHA-256 `digests` of each shipped file.

  value); foot slide measured on sole vertices of the exported file stays near zero in stance.
